How can we help?

Contact us for help with signing in, squads, players, match results, privacy, or account deletion.

Email support

Team Master is operated by Mark Millsopp.

refuelr@outlook.com

Use “Team Master” in the subject so we can identify the app. You can also copy the address into your email app.

What to include

  • What happened and what you expected.
  • Your app version, device, and the approximate time of the problem.
  • A screenshot if useful, with other players’ personal details hidden.

Never send your password, email verification code, or account deletion receipt.

Problems signing in

Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen. If you are creating an account, confirm your email using the code sent to you. Contact support if you cannot access your email or the reset does not work.

Delete your account

  1. Open the Team Master iPhone app and sign in.
  2. Open More > Delete account.
  3. Read what will be removed, type DELETE, and confirm.
  4. Check Account deletion status on that device for completion. The status page is also available from the welcome screen.

Cleanup normally completes within 24 hours. Your account and access are removed, along with account-linked personal details. Squads with no remaining owner are deleted; shared squads remain available to their other owners.

If deletion still shows pending after 24 hours, reports a problem, or you cannot use the in-app process, email support. Include the email address used for your account and when you requested deletion. We may need to verify account ownership. Do not send your deletion receipt.

Privacy and other data requests

Read the Team Master privacy policy for information about data use, retention, and your rights. To request help with player details entered under a different email address, explain which squad or record needs attention.
