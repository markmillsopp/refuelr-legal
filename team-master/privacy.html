Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

This policy covers Team Master’s iPhone app, web app, and account service. It does not describe the separate Refuelr fuel-price app.

Who is responsible

Mark Millsopp operates Team Master and is the data controller for the service. For privacy questions or requests, email refuelr@outlook.com.

Information we use

  • Account information: first name, last name, email address, account identifier, and authentication information managed by Amazon Cognito.
  • Team information: squad names and ownership, player names, optional player email addresses, availability, team selections, match dates and locations, scores, and performance statistics. Squad owners may enter information about other players.
  • Technical information: security, request, error, and diagnostic records needed to operate and protect the service.
  • Support correspondence: the information you choose to send when contacting us.

Match locations are entered as part of organising games; they are not a record of continuous device-location tracking.

Why we use it

We use account and team information as necessary to perform our contract with you and provide the service you request: signing in, managing and sharing squads, balancing teams, and recording results. Required account fields are needed to create and manage your account; player email addresses are optional.

We use limited technical information and support correspondence for our legitimate interests in security, reliability, abuse prevention, and answering requests. We may also process information where necessary to meet a legal obligation. We do not sell personal information. Team Master does not use advertising or third-party tracking.

Who can receive it

Other owners of a shared squad can see its players, games, results, and ownership information. Only share player details that you have permission to use.

Amazon Web Services provides authentication, databases, hosting, and operational infrastructure. The main service region is Ireland (eu-west-1). Microsoft processes support email sent to the address above.

Service providers process information under their applicable terms and our service arrangements. Some processing may occur outside your country. Where required, international transfers must use applicable adequacy arrangements or contractual safeguards. Contact us for information about the safeguards relevant to your data.

How long we keep it

We keep account and team information while it is needed to provide Team Master. Operational records are retained as needed for security, troubleshooting, and legal obligations.

After account deletion completes, the service keeps a minimal pseudonymous completion record for seven days. An encrypted deletion receipt stays on the requesting device until you remove it from the status page. Opaque erased-record identifiers are retained to prevent older app data from restoring deleted player details.

Account and data deletion

In the Team Master iPhone app, open More > Delete account and confirm the request. Deletion removes your account, squad access, and account-linked personal details.

  • A squad with no other owner is deleted together with its players and games.
  • If other owners remain, their shared squad and match records stay available. Your ownership record and account-linked player details are removed, including linked player snapshots in matches.
  • Manually entered details that use a different email may not be automatically recognised. Contact support to have those records located.

Deletion normally completes within 24 hours. Check Account deletion status on the device that requested deletion for confirmation. If you cannot access your account or need help with other personal data, use the support page.

Your rights

Depending on the law that applies, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of your personal information. Email refuelr@outlook.com. We may need to verify your identity before providing or changing personal data.

Your right to object

You may object to processing based on our legitimate interests, including security and support processing, on grounds relating to your particular situation. Contact us using the same email address.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

Security and policy changes

We use access controls, encrypted connections, and managed cloud services to protect information. No service can guarantee absolute security. Material changes to this policy will be reflected here and in the app with an updated date.
