Delete your Refuelr account

Effective: 16 July 2026

In-app deletion

You can delete your own account inside the Refuelr app at Manage account > Delete account.

That action permanently deletes your account and associated Refuelr data. It does not cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription.

How to request deletion

  1. Open Refuelr and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Manage account.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm the deletion request when prompted.

If you cannot access the app, email refuelr@outlook.com from the email address linked to your Refuelr account and ask for account deletion.

What we delete

  • Your Firebase account, Refuelr profile, and sign-in data stored by Refuelr.
  • Your RevenueCat customer profile and purchase history.
  • Account-backed saved routes, favourites, search-usage records, and account settings.
  • Notification tokens and price alerts.
  • Community fuel-price and heating-oil submissions linked to your user ID.
  • Daily anti-abuse records and route-usage records linked to your user ID.
  • Your user ID from other community confirmations and reports.

What we keep

  • Unlinked shared route-cache entries may remain for up to 7 days.
  • Limited diagnostic, security, or operational logs may remain for the configured provider retention period.
  • Billing records retained by Apple or Google Play for billing, legal, tax, fraud-prevention, or chargeback purposes.

Retention period

Account-linked data is removed from active Refuelr systems when deletion completes. The limited records above follow their stated or provider-controlled retention periods.

Subscriptions

Deleting Refuelr does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it separately in Apple App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

Contact

Questions about deletion or privacy? Contact refuelr@outlook.com.
