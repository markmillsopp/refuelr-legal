Data sources

Official UK fuel data in Refuelr is refreshed every 30 minutes through the UK Government Fuel Finder feed. Refuelr now covers 11k+ stations, including 7k+ official stations, with more joining every day. UK stations not yet in the Fuel Finder scheme are covered by community-submitted prices until they join. Republic of Ireland fuel prices currently rely on community submissions. Home heating oil uses official and community data across the island of Ireland, and community-submitted data across the UK.
