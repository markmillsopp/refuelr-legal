TripWire

Support

TripWire is a private family gate alert system. It requires a TripWire account and compatible gate hardware.

Account access

Accounts are created privately. Contact the family member who provided your TripWire login if access stops working.

Pairing help

  1. Power the gate sensor near the router.
  2. Confirm the home network supports 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi.
  3. Open TripWire and choose Pair device.
  4. Keep TripWire open until setup finishes.

If pairing fails, reconnect the iPhone to the home network. Then reset the sensor and try again.
